# Sparse site occupancy on a shared scaffold

> Use MuseDraw to draw three matching views of one two-strand synthetic scaffold: Initial, Paired at q and Redistributed. Keep strand identities, geometry and the three sites p/q/r fixed. Initially p marks the dark strand and q the light strand; then q marks both; finally remove p and add a dark-strand mark at r while q remains paired. Show the differences on the scaffold, with sparse warm markers and no process cards.

Original qualitative drawing concept. Crossbars are illustrative geometry, not nucleotide lengths. No biological mechanism, quantitative abundance or experimental result is claimed. Markers identify the supplied occupancy state; their areas do not encode measurements. One canonical scientific object is reused in three views, with sparse site-state overrides.
