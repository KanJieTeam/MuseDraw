# Design decisions

Setbacks and connected stair landings turn a stack of planes into an inhabited spatial sequence.
Each floor has a different use. Cast shadows and furniture share the projection; the blue/cream palette has small ochre book accents. This remains a concept illustration, not a construction plan.

Original vector interpretation of a visual language in the author-supplied MuseDraw brand moodboard. All shapes and text are editable. This is an art-directed scene, not a promise that a short prompt reproduces the same composition. No external images or fonts are embedded.
