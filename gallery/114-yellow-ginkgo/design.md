# Design decisions

Oversized asymmetric fan shapes create the structure; fine veins add a quieter second scale.
The composition crosses the frame deliberately. Yellow negative space connects the title to the lower stems; the illustrated fans are stylized, not a botanical identification plate.

Original vector interpretation of a visual language in the author-supplied MuseDraw brand moodboard. All shapes and text are editable. This is an art-directed scene, not a promise that a short prompt reproduces the same composition. No external images or fonts are embedded.
