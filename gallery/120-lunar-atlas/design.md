# Design decisions

One large split disc carries contrast; a narrow eight-symbol strip introduces sequence without competing for focus.
All phase symbols are schematic graphics. No observed surface data, dates or astronomical measurements are asserted.

Original vector interpretation of a visual language in the author-supplied MuseDraw brand moodboard. All shapes and text are editable. This is an art-directed scene, not a promise that a short prompt reproduces the same composition. No external images or fonts are embedded.

## Revision 3

Cluster crater detail into highlands and leave quieter plains; avoid a uniform bubble pattern across the disc. Preserve the eight-symbol sequence and measuring grid.
