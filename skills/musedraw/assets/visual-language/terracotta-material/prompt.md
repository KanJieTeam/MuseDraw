# Mend what matters

Use MuseDraw's Terracotta family for an English community mending event.
Make the fabric patch and worn wood carry the idea of care. Compose exact,
editable typography beside a rich full-height original photographic asset.
Keep the clay/olive relationship and quiet serif voice. Include 08 November 2026,
Northbank Community Workshop, and two selectable local demonstration sessions:
10:00–11:30 and 14:00–15:30. This is a fictional event, not a real booking service.

The saved `workshop.png` is an original generated raster asset. Replaying this
case uses that saved image; creating new imagery from `asset-prompt.txt` needs a
host image-generation tool and will vary. The photograph is illustrative,
not evidence of a real workshop or a technical repair tutorial.
