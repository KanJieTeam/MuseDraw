# Mend what matters

This Terracotta composition makes the original patched linen and worn wood the
main image. Large quiet serif type occupies another field; clay, olive and warm
paper are carried by real depicted materials rather than a row of coloured cards.
The patch makes the event's subject visible before the supporting copy is read.

The full-height image needs a different crop at a narrow width. Keep the patch
and recognisable stool visible; typography and visit information remain native
HTML instead of being painted into the image. The two visit choices update their
selected state and the local confirmation. This fictional event has no booking
backend. Do not present the generated photograph as evidence of a real event.

Copy the whole folder and open `index.html`. The saved `workshop.png` reproduces
this asset without an image-generation service. Read `asset-prompt.txt` only when
new raster material is needed; fresh generation requires the host's image tool
and can vary. This work was rendered and self-reviewed locally, not independently
ranked or tested as a new generation on another model.
