# Explicit story scaffolds and local sorting catalogues

`story-scaffold` turns supplied writing elements into a traceable sentence sequence. `sorting-catalogue` draws local catalogue buckets and checks supplied practice answers. Neither infers unseen facts from a name or image.

- Defaults: `assets/musecore/story-scaffold.json` and `sorting-catalogue.json`.
- The writing scaffold accepts two to six elements and an explicit permutation. Example, caller-located visible content, imagination and unknown content retain different identities. Visible content requires the supplied material reference and location; no image means no visible claim.
- References are retained, not fetched, embedded or interpreted as image recognition. Unknown content remains unknown; the programme does not invent an event to complete a sentence.
- Sorting accepts two to six categories, zero to 24 explicit catalogue items and optional answers. Each item has a declared category or null. Unlisted/unknown items do not silently enter an other category.
- Jurisdiction, version and HTTPS catalogue source are explicit. The default refers to the Beijing park guidance example; its source-inspired colours are design colours, not a claim to reproduce the full official sign standard.
- Wrong practice answers are compared only with the supplied catalogue. No name-based classification, legal compliance, recycling-service availability or educational effect is inferred.
- Reject invalid references/permutations/category IDs and unreadable overflow. Preserve complete action wording and uncertainty rather than shortening it into an apparently certain result.

Use normal core rendering and inspect the page at delivery size. These are bounded development compositions; neither a passed answer check nor a source link proves aesthetic quality.
