# Declared practice stages and proportional lesson timing

These warm-clay teaching compositions preserve time and responsibilities instead of turning all activities into equal cards.

## Weekly practice

- Start from `assets/musecore/weekly-practice.json`: two to six stages, each one to three weeks, no more than 12 weeks in total.
- Stage goals, observable exercises and role suggestions are supplied plan content. Relative week/day ranges are calculated; no calendar date is invented when the start date is absent.
- An explicit ISO start date allows Gregorian date ranges, including month/year boundaries and leap days. These ranges do not infer holidays, school terms or workday policies.
- Goals such as a supplied practice duration are examples, not developmental norms or evidence of an individual child's adaptation. No psychological diagnosis or educational-effect result is inferred.

## Lesson timing

- Start from `assets/musecore/lesson-timing.json`: one to eight intervals, one to four responsibility roles, total duration five to 180 minutes.
- Every interval consumes its declared duration, including an explicitly named buffer. The continuous bar uses the same minute scale; role actions align to the same intervals.
- Selected-time totals and shares depend only on explicit selection flags, never guessed from an activity title. No selected interval means zero selected minutes.
- Reject duration-budget inconsistencies, unknown roles and unreadable excess. Missing action/empty buffer stays explicit, rather than being filled with invented instruction.

Use normal core rendering. Supplied notes remain visible; model/scope metadata is not a substitute for the reader's diagram. Review the final whole page and intended delivery-size preview; a passed schedule check is not proof of teaching quality or aesthetics.
