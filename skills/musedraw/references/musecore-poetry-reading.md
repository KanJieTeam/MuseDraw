# Poetry pages and declared literary readings

These two bounded compositions transfer different page decisions: ink wash and right-to-left verse for `moon-poem`; a continuous red reading path and attributed quotations for `literary-reading-arc`. They do not turn a supplied interpretation into a quantitative measurement.

- Start from `assets/musecore/moon-poem.json` or `assets/musecore/literary-reading-arc.json`.
- The known Jingyesi edition validates its four complete lines and attribution. Other poems require supplied-poem mode with two to eight bounded lines; that mode does not authenticate authorship.
- The reading arc accepts two to seven nodes. Upper/middle/lower placement and reading labels are supplied interpretive choices, not inferred emotion scores. Quotations from the finite Chibifu catalogue retain their source passage and speaker context.
- Measured verse, annotation boxes, moon diameter and branch extent determine space. Reading-node labels remain attached to their path positions; long labels grow the page rather than shrink text.
- The supplied note remains visible. The moon, frost strokes, river and washes are illustrative, not evidence about a real scene or psychological state.
- Exit for text outside the supported catalogue/declared-text contract, unsupported counts or unreadable canvas size. Do not silently abbreviate quotations or force extra nodes into old positions.

Render with the normal `musedraw.mjs core render` command. Keep the resulting SVG/PNG and content input together. Check the full page and actual delivery-size preview; passing geometry does not establish aesthetic quality.
