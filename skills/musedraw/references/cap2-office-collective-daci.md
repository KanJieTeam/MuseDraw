# Explicit collective DACI declarations

Optional `collectives` adds contributor/informed group declarations to the existing `daci` family. Exact individual assignments continue to use the existing `daciAssignments` validator and deduplication. Driver and approver must each retain exactly one explicit person ID. Contributor and informed roles may contain explicitly named individuals, groups, or both.

`collectives` accepts only `contributors` and `informed` arrays, at most six declarations per role and one to ten overall. Each declaration needs a globally unique declaration `id`, complete `label`, explicit `count`, and complete `note`. `count:null` means not given. A safe integer from 0 to 1e9 is a supplied count, including an explicit zero. Missing count refuses. Identical or different labels never establish identity, disjointness, or overlap. Each declaration ID identifies an annotation, not a deduplicated person or group.

The measured four-role view labels individual-list counts separately and retains full named individuals, full group labels, given/unknown counts, group notes, role responsibilities and caller note. Empty individual lists mean no identities were supplied; they do not mean the role has no participants. Group counts are declared membership counts and may overlap other groups or individuals. The model exposes given membership sums when all relevant counts are supplied, but never computes unique combined headcount. Its `uniqueCombinedPeople` is always null. The page explicitly explains this boundary.

The source input retains o03's named driver, unnamed-but-explicit CFO approver, contributor label “IT、法务、两位部门助理”, and “全体员工”. The source plural count is “若干人”; no complete roster or total is supplied. Two department assistants remain visible in the label without inventing IT/legal identities or a company headcount. Original responsibility phrases and common-error note remain supplied text. The source identity is a composition reference; it is not a governance recommendation or actual organizational record.

Integration imports `cap2OfficeCollectiveDaciFigure` into `responsibility-figures.mjs` and guards `daci(input)` with `if(Object.hasOwn(input,'collectives'))return cap2OfficeCollectiveDaciFigure(input);`. Bundle the new model after `responsibility-models.mjs`, and the new figure after the visual helpers and new model, before `responsibility-figures.mjs`. Inputs without the field stay on the existing route.

Fixed reading sizes and measured page growth are bounded; excessive text refuses without truncation. Passing does not establish aesthetic approval.
