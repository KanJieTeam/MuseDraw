# Supplied plant stages and matter/energy paths

`bean-cycle` accepts five to seven ordered common-bean teaching stages from a
bounded morphology catalogue. Seed, radicle, seedling, flower and fruit are
explicit primary stages. Leaf, flower, pod and seed marks are supplied schematic
counts, not biological measurements. Supplied elapsed days annotate observations
or teaching examples; layout spacing does not predict growth rate, flowering or
yield. The return arrow means seeds of the next generation, not reverse growth
of the same plant.

`leaf-energy` compares the overall oxygenic photosynthetic process with aerobic
respiration in a living green leaf with oxygen explicitly available. Matter and
energy have different paths. Supplied light/no-light teaching conditions control
whether the conceptual overall photosynthetic path is shown; respiration is
retained in either case. Glycolysis includes the cytosol, so respiration is not
assigned wholly to mitochondria. No flux, stoichiometry, ATP yield, gas-exchange
rate or actual process activity is inferred from a line or mark count.

Use `core example bean-cycle` or `core example leaf-energy`, inspect the explicit
catalogue/schema, then `core render`. Objects, complete label boxes and paths
reflow together. Other species, fermentation, CAM or unsupported biological
processes require another route. Test success does not validate a biological
prediction or approve the resulting appearance.
