# Ideal Grover arithmetic and synthetic primer geometry

Two narrow teaching compositions share typography tools, not scientific semantics. Neither executes a quantum device nor validates an experimental primer pair.

## Grover amplitudes

- Start from `assets/musecore/grover-amplitudes.json`: two or three qubits, one explicit marked bit string and zero to three iterations.
- A noiseless uniform real state vector is updated at every oracle/diffusion step. Exact rational coefficients and probabilities accompany the displayed signed amplitudes.
- The circuit operations use the same model. The top wire is the displayed bit string's most significant bit. The global phase correction is explicit, so signs match the stated diffusion convention.
- The first-lobe peak is specific to this ideal single-target model. Multi-Z is abstract, not a hardware elementary-gate decomposition.
- Refuse unsupported qubit/target/iteration shapes. No noisy-device performance, measurement samples, database execution or cryptanalytic result is implied.

## Primer pair

- Start from `assets/musecore/primer-pair.json`. Only explicitly synthetic teaching templates of 60 to 360 A/C/G/T bases and supplied 12 to 30 nt primers are supported.
- Binding spans are 1-based inclusive. Forward matches the displayed top strand; reverse is its stated span's reverse complement. Disjoint sites face inward; product span includes both sites.
- Length, GC, literal matching occurrences and antiparallel contiguous complementary runs are calculated. Repeated matches are not called unique. The supplied reference bands are descriptive, not acceptance guarantees.
- The paired-base diagram shows literal geometry. It does not compute thermodynamic dimers, hairpins, melting/annealing temperatures, specificity or experimental suitability. Tm remains explicitly uncomputed.
- Reject inconsistent spans/orientation/sequences and unsupported reference bands. Do not silently redesign a primer or infer salt/concentration assumptions.

Use normal core rendering and retain the teaching input. Inspect both full and delivery-size images. The runtime checks facts and geometry within scope; visual quality still needs a separate judgement.
