# Seven-step hand-rubbing teaching pages

`handwash-practice` retains a soft two-column teaching page with large mnemonic characters and contact-region drawings. It separates a rubbing lesson from the complete washing process.

- Default: `assets/musecore/handwash-practice.json`. Select one to seven entries from the closed seven-step catalogue, retaining their original catalogue numbers and full actions.
- The simplified hand/contact drawings are explanatory objects, not action recognition, clinical instructions tailored to a person or proof that an action was completed correctly.
- Catalogue mode has no computed practice duration. Practice-plan mode totals explicitly supplied seconds and rounds as a classroom plan; the total is not an actual cleaning duration, efficacy result or recommended threshold.
- The default rubbing reminder is explicitly about the rubbing portion. Wetting, applying soap, rubbing, rinsing and drying remain a distinct full-process reminder. A whole-process duration from another source is not relabelled as seven-step rubbing time.
- Preserve the supplied official teaching-source links and distinction between source variants. Selecting a subset does not mean the complete wash has been performed.
- Reject unknown steps, reordered/duplicate catalogue identities, inconsistent timing modes, invalid counts or unreadable excess. Do not infer health outcomes or fill in an unobserved action.

Use the normal core renderer and review both the full page and intended delivery-size image. Source checking and geometry do not themselves prove visual quality or real hygiene performance.
