# Multiplication practice and a declared flipped-classroom week

The two compositions retain different reading tasks: a neon S-shaped catalogue route for `multiplication-quest`, and an open numbered week for `flipped-week`.

- Defaults: `assets/musecore/multiplication-quest.json`, `flipped-week.json`.
- Multiplication uses the bounded small-nine triangular catalogue through a supplied terminal factor from two to nine. Completed whole gates and current-gate registrations are explicit records, not inferred mastery or automatic unlocking.
- Formula counts and products are computed from the catalogue. Partial registration retains its given status; a fully marked gate does not establish real recall, learning or a test score.
- The classroom week accepts three to seven consecutive explicitly dated days with ordered pre/in/post phases. Supplied activities, durations and group population remain distinct.
- Group sizes are calculated from the given participant count. Only quantities sharing a unit are added; video minutes, class minutes and counts are not merged into a fictitious single total.
- Plans are plans: no completion, engagement or learning result is inferred. The runtime does not recreate unrelated questions merely because the source page mentioned them.
- Reject unsupported catalogue IDs, contradictory registrations, dates, phase order and capacity excess. Rebuild object positions from admitted content; do not overwrite source facts to fit a route.
