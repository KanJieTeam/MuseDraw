# Visual revision with a separate critic

Use when the user requests sustained visual improvement, a design comparison or
critique of a rendered work. This is an experimental production method, not a
promise of better taste and not an extra gate for every ordinary diagram.
Use the medium's existing production route. A larger agent team is not itself
an improvement.

## Build and judge different things

The creator receives the actual brief, supplied facts and assets, relevant Skill
route and editing constraints. Before a new composition, decide what the material
does in the space: title/image relationship, reading path and change at the final
viewing size. A filled-out plan alone does not establish a distinctive result.

Keep the first source and rendered output. Run factual and functional checks
separately from visual judgement. A screenshot cannot prove that a form works;
an error-free browser cannot prove that the composition is good.

Where the host supports independent contexts, give a new critic only:

- the unchanged user brief and required facts;
- actual same-scale renders, including the relevant narrow/detail view;
- a supplied design system or authorised visual reference, if one applies.

Exclude the creator's reasoning, arm identity, previous scores and the claim that
the revision should win. The critic reports a visible problem, its location and
effect, a possible causal change, and the strongest part to preserve. Ask for
uncertainty when the image is insufficient. Do not treat its preferred font or
palette as a defect. If independent contexts are unavailable, use an honest
self-review; do not label it independent or stop usable work from being delivered.

## Revise without erasing the useful part

Separate verifiable defects from taste preferences. Repeated dates, an unreadable
label, a missing fact or an empty panel can have direct evidence. “More premium”
and “not enough energy” need an observable explanation before becoming edits.
Ask what the proposed change improves for this brief.

Choose a small number of high-impact causes rather than applying every comment.
Preserve distinctive successful choices. Save revisions in a new directory and
check the actual output again; adding a caption, for example, can introduce a
collision even when its absence was a reasonable critique.

Compare a proposed revision against the best existing version without showing
which is newer. Accept it when facts and working behaviour are preserved and the
visual evidence supports the change. Keep the earlier version on a tie,
uncertainty or regression; later is not automatically better.

If critics disagree, record the disagreement. A further vote is not ground truth.
Resolve checkable issues first and leave subjective alternatives available for
the user. Repeatedly making a title larger or reducing content can satisfy a
critic while weakening the actual work. When the same important gap survives
successive edits, revisit composition, asset choice or the production medium
instead of repeating cosmetic adjustments. Respect the user's continuing request
and any stated budget; report unavailable tools or unresolved limits honestly.

## Learn a method, not a victory count

A method change needs trials on new content. Keep development subjects separate
from unseen tasks; a task used to adjust a rule becomes a regression example.
Record failures, creator/critic calls and revision effort as well as preference.
For a comparison campaign, one content package with two real works is one pair;
viewports, repeated judges and edits do not multiply it.

Initial poster and website development trials exposed disagreement between
independent model critics and similar compositions despite explicit direction
notes. Those observations motivate this separation and rollback discipline;
they do not establish an aesthetic advantage. Use actual results before adding
further roles, scoring rules or default work for every user.

[dream-loop](https://github.com/achimala/dream-loop), and
[Anthropic evaluator-optimizer workflows](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents).
These explain possible mechanisms, not independent proof of MuseDraw's quality.
