These teaching compositions display explicit records and assumptions. They do not perform scientific identification from an illustrative picture.

- Start from `assets/musecore/cryoem-evidence.json` with explicit processing stages, selection counts and FSC samples. The readout threshold is fixed at 0.143; it is not a configurable input.
- The count ledger retains the given selection relationships. FSC crossings follow the supplied sampled segments; missing records remain missing rather than becoming zero or a smooth invented curve.

## Collider event

- Start from `assets/musecore/collider-event.json`. Known particle objects and an ideal uniform axial magnetic field are declared inputs, not inferred detector classifications.
- Circular trajectories and transverse momentum vectors use those same inputs. Very small curling tracks below the admitted diagram scale reject; they are not secretly magnified.
- Detector layers and signal matrix describe the supplied teaching event. No real detector return field, noise, material interaction, particle identification or complete detector simulation is computed.
- An imbalance in visible momentum is not uniquely attributed to a neutrino. Missing information and simplified geometry do not become a new physical discovery.
